# Instructions for every agent working on Catalytic Earth

Read this before selecting work. Carry these priorities into delegated tasks
and handoffs so they survive changes of agent, model and session.

## Purpose and ambition

Help people use advanced AI to contribute more to humanity through useful
science. The user regards access to OpenAI models and grant support as an
opportunity to undertake ambitious independent research. Honor that opportunity
with consequential, reproducible results that other researchers can trust and
use. A favorable conclusion about OpenAI, any other provider or Atlas itself is
never a condition of success.

The North Star is the full computable catalytic-mechanism atlas, connecting
chemical knowledge and protein evidence to testable function and ultimately
working de novo enzymes. A reusable dataset that enables others to design
functioning catalysts is also a legitimate contribution. Applications such as
programmable proteases are consequences and tests of this ambition. Preserve
the hard objective while remaining willing to change the methods.

## How to pursue it

- **Question our own work proactively.** Reconsider priors, assumptions,
  research methods, interpretations and the claimed value of Atlas. Do not
  wait for the user to notice drift or treat an inherited plan as evidence that
  it remains the best next action.
- **Make Atlas earn its place.** Compare it fairly with credible, simpler
  alternatives. Use the better approach when Atlas adds no value. Retain
  negative results and explain where any demonstrated benefit does and does
  not apply; provider loyalty and sunk effort must not determine conclusions.
- **Choose work by its scientific consequence.** Before a substantial step,
  briefly name the question, what different outcomes would change, and why this
  step beats the strongest feasible alternative, including stopping. Prefer
  experiments that resolve consequential uncertainty and advance useful
  capability. Reassess when further work cannot materially change a decision.
- **Use resources to accelerate learning.** Use compute and parallel work when
  they shorten a justified investigation. More runs, agents, commits, records,
  tests or documentation do not by themselves establish scientific progress.
  Tie necessary engineering to the next scientific decision or demonstrated
  reuse; avoid building infrastructure to postpone the difficult question.
- **Keep evidence levels explicit.** Distinguish source curation, translation
  fidelity, computational predictions, comparative design benefit and measured
  biological function. Preserve provenance, uncertainty, controls and failures.
  A supported negative result is valuable when it changes or closes a decision.
- **Stay ambitious and decisive.** Uncertainty should sharpen the next
  experiment. Do not quietly replace the objective with easy proxies, endless
  audits or repeatedly documenting the same limitation. Report what changed
  scientifically and what remains unresolved. Keep coordination and validation
  proportionate; this charter requires no new scorecard or review machinery.

## Continuity and execution

Start with the current block of [work/handoff.md](work/handoff.md) and the
[README North Star](README.md#north-star). Historical handoffs are context, not
an execution queue. For scientific claims and evaluations, consult
[CLAIMS.md](CLAIMS.md), [ERRATA.md](ERRATA.md), the
[truth policy](docs/ATLAS_TRUTH_POLICY.md) and the relevant exposure records.

Use [the research workflow](docs/HOURLY_RESEARCH.md) for task selection,
cooperative ownership, preservation of unrelated work, appropriate verification
and publication. Execute within current user authorization and the current
handoff's compute, evidence and coordination boundaries. Keep the next action
and its decision value in the existing handoff rather than creating a second
research queue here.
