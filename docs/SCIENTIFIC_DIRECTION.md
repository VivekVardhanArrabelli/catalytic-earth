### Generalization: demonstrated computation and remaining manual work

1. **Bespoke code:** the inner fragment and reference-feature rules operate on
   source labels, bonds, numbering and references. Their demonstrated reuse is
   His/Glu/Lys within one M0187 entry. The public CLI still supplies M0187
   explicitly; broad cross-chemistry fragment-query reuse is untested. Other
   functional additions reuse the perturbation consumer. The AHAS inference
   and CODH assessment extensions represent distinct scientific concepts,
   with narrow demonstrated scope. Absence of enzyme IDs in an inner function
   alone is insufficient evidence of a general system.
2. **Reusable curation:** existing providers, graph parsers and measurements
   serve new relations without transcription. However, source selection,
   identity declarations, assay applicability and mechanistic interpretation
   still need manual review. AHAS `inference_basis`/`source_equations` and CODH's
   supported assessment are reviewed declarations; the consumer checks their
   structure and provenance rather than independently deriving their scientific
   meaning. Storing bespoke reasoning in JSON does not establish generalization
   or a measured reduction in curation effort.
3. **Integrated value:** the new relations prevent specific unsupported
   transfers and retain positive product/function information. One important
   join remains unfinished: E317's fragment query has no matching original
   focal observation, while the newer primary factors are retrieved separately
   through the perturbation query. Both carry reference-site/feature/citation
   information, but a generic atom-to-primary-outcome answer is not assembled.
   Empty-match semantics correctly avoid a false absence claim. This is a real
   integration limit, not evidence that E317 has no functional data. No complete
   design-ready chain or demonstrated design performance is established.

These limits guide the choice of consequential future relations; they do not

Do not build another case-specific reporting path. Reassess the strongest next
