# M0187 transformation source attribution

This directory retains three byte-for-byte official responses used to audit the bounded M0187 Step 1 substrate-to-intermediate transformation: the current Rhea master-reaction TSV for [RHEA:13945](https://www.rhea-db.org/rhea/13945) and the Rhea-hosted ChEBI structures for [CHEBI:17756](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/searchId.do?chebiId=CHEBI:17756) and [CHEBI:32382](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/searchId.do?chebiId=CHEBI:32382). The acquisition ledger records all three requests, response headers, byte counts, hashes and timestamps. It retains the original temporary capture paths as historical receipt facts; `source_inventory.json` maps each response to its repository path.

[Rhea data are licensed under CC BY 4.0](https://www.rhea-db.org/help/license-disclaimer); credit Rhea and identify the retrieval date. The participant structures retain ChEBI identifiers and should also credit [ChEBI/EMBL-EBI under CC BY 4.0](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/ontologies/chebi). The reused [M-CSA source is CC BY 4.0](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/thornton-srv/m-csa/) and remains at its existing repository path.

The official files provide reaction and molecular structures, not an atom map between participants or M-CSA panels. The retained M0187 Step 1 drawing has an R-configured, net-minus-one mandelate graph but partially labels it `chebi:17756`; later panels omit the product. Project-authored graph correspondence must preserve that label conflict, phenyl-ring symmetry, the record-versus-proposal direction conflict, and the absence of a drawn terminal S product. These sources do not support a complete R-to-S trajectory, physiological direction or experimental validation.
