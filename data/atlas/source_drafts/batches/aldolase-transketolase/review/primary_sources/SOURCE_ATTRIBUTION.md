# Primary source attribution for the M0222 audit

This directory preserves one RCSB Protein Data Bank entry as the direct structural source for the narrow rabbit aldolase DHAP-enamine claim.

- **Structure:** RCSB PDB [2QUT](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/2QUT), DOI [10.2210/pdb2qut/pdb](https://doi.org/10.2210/pdb2qut/pdb). The canonical mmCIF access URL is <https://files.rcsb.org/download/2QUT.cif>.
- **Primary citation:** Marc-Olivier St-Jean and Jurgen Sygusch, “Stereospecific Proton Transfer by a Mobile Catalyst in Mammalian Fructose-1,6-bisphosphate Aldolase,” *Journal of Biological Chemistry* 282 (2007), 31028–31037, DOI [10.1074/jbc.M704968200](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M704968200), [PubMed 17728250](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17728250/).
- **Data terms:** PDB archive data are made available under the [CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); see the [RCSB PDB usage policy](https://www.rcsb.org/pages/policies). Attribution to the structure authors and primary citation is retained here even though CC0 does not require it.

`2QUT.cif` was copied byte-for-byte from an existing research file at `/tmp/2QUT.cif`. The earlier HTTP request URL, status, headers, and retrieval receipt were not preserved. `source_inventory.json` therefore records the official canonical URL only as an identity and access link, not as a reconstructed acquisition receipt.

`evidence_projection.json` is a project-authored extraction tied to the exact raw-file SHA-256. It is limited to deposited identity, chain-A UniProt alignment, and the deposited covalent connection. It does not redistribute article or UniProt content and does not establish reaction direction, direct observation of free-substrate attack, the complete M-CSA mechanism, or archaeal applicability.
